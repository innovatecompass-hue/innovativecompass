# Astrology Journal: Vela & Lyra social posts

Mascots, realistic and futuristic, dry and knowing:
- **Vela:** tall, pearlescent lavender skin, elongated cranium traced with rose-gold constellation lines, iridescent opal robe. The calm one who has read every chart.
- **Lyra:** teal-silver iridescent skin, silver hair sculpted into a crescent moon, gold planet ear cuffs, constellation cape. Curious about humans, usually confused by them.

Saved in Higgsfield as the Element **Vela-and-Lyra** (`17fed39a-fe7f-47b3-816b-1815cb851e85`). Character design job: `9d46bfbc-956b-4cdd-9d84-37faa51b8925`.

**How to make more:** use GPT Image 2.5 (quality high, 2k, 4:5) with the design job as an image reference. It renders the speech-bubble text cleanly. For video, the Element works in Seedance 2.0 and Kling 3.0.

Format: 4:5, for Instagram and Facebook feeds. Two white speech bubbles, black serif text. Every caption sends people to the free journal (link in bio). Tag the bio link `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=vela-lyra`.
