Three principles in three columns, each a `principle` title in Playfair under a `taupe-35` hairline.

```html

<div class="manifesto">
  <div class="manifesto-item">
    <span class="num">01</span>
    <strong>Systems should disappear.</strong>
    <p>…</p>
  </div>
  …
</div>
```

- **You provide:** three principles, each with a short declarative title and one or two sentences that make it concrete.
- **Style:** the number is `index-label` in `gold`, the title `principle` (22px Playfair 400), and the copy `body-xs` in `ink-muted`.
- **Layout:** three columns with `space-72` gaps; one column under 960px.
