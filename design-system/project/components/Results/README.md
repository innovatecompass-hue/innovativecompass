# Results

Three outcome figures on `ink`, each a huge `stat` numeral in `gold-soft` over a 40px `gold` rule and a short caption, with an italic caveat below.

```html
<div class="results-grid">
  <div class="result">
    <div class="big">20+</div>
    <div class="rule"></div>
    <p>Hours returned to founders each week.</p>
  </div>
  …
</div>
<p class="results-caveat">Figures reflect typical outcomes across client engagements in their first 90 days.</p>
```

- **You provide:** exactly three figures, each with a caption of 22ch or less, and a caveat that says where the numbers come from.
- **Style:** captions in `body-sm` (`on-dark-78`); the caveat in `aside` (`taupe`, 6.9:1 on ink).
- **Only use figures you can stand behind,** and always include the caveat.
