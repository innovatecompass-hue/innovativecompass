A Note from the Founder

I started this firm because I kept meeting the same founder.

Smart. Building something real. And quietly drowning in the back-office version of their own business — tools that didn't talk to each other, work they couldn't seem to hand off, decisions that kept getting postponed because the numbers weren't in one place.

I realized the problem wasn't that they needed more software. It was that no one had sat with them long enough to understand what they actually needed. So that's what we do first. Always.

Miranda Ogletree Founder · Innovative Compass
